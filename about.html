About OrderBox

Online Ordering That Doesn't Take a Cut

OrderBox is flat-fee online ordering for independent restaurants running their own drivers. Customers order from the restaurant's own website, and each order prints straight to the in-store printer, ready for the kitchen. Restaurants pay £99 a month, with no commission and no per-order fees.

Why We Started OrderBox

OrderBox started because we saw the pressure delivery platforms have been putting on independent restaurant owners in villages and market towns across England. We don't think it's fair to take a share of a restaurant's revenue through commission without delivering everything that's promised in return. So we built a simpler alternative, and we want to change how this market works.
