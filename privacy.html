Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains how OrderBox Ltd uses personal data collected through this website, orderbox.me. It doesn't cover the ordering websites of individual restaurants.

Who We Are

OrderBox Ltd is the data controller for this website. We're registered in England and Wales, company number 17214544. Our registered office is 21 Sperling Drive, Haverhill, England, CB9 9SG. You can contact us at info@orderbox.me.

What We Collect

When you complete our enquiry form or email us, we collect the details you give us:

  • Your name
  • Your restaurant's name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if you provide it
  • Anything you include in your message

Like most websites, our service providers also process technical information, such as your IP address, when you visit, so that the site can be delivered and kept secure.

How We Use It

We use your details to reply to your enquiry and to talk to you about OrderBox. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.

We don't sell your personal data.

Who We Share It With

We use these service providers to run this website:

  • Formspree receives and delivers enquiry form submissions
  • Cloudflare delivers and protects the website
  • Google Cloud hosts the website
  • jsDelivr delivers the code for the chart on our homepage

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. We may also share information if the law requires it.

How Long We Keep It

We keep your details only for as long as we need them for the purposes above.

Cookies and Local Storage

Our website code doesn't use analytics or advertising cookies. If you use the dark mode button, your choice is saved in your browser so it's remembered on your next visit. Cloudflare may set cookies that are needed for security.

Your Rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your personal data, and to ask us to correct or delete it. You can also object to how we use it, or ask us to restrict it. To make a request, email info@orderbox.me.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
