Why We Built This

You Take All the Risk. You Should Keep All the Profit.

We think commission is broken for restaurants that already do the hard part themselves. You lease the building. You hire the staff. You buy the stock. You pay for your own drivers and your own vehicles. If a delivery goes wrong, it's your name on it — not a platform's. So when a percentage of every order gets skimmed off the top, forever, that's not a fee for a service rendered — it's rent charged on risk somebody else took. We built OrderBox on the opposite idea: one flat fee for the software, and then we get out of the way. No commission, no percentage, no cut of your growth. If you have a great month, that's yours.
